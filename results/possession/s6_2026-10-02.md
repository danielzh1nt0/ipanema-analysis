# S6 - sequences flicker (2 Oct, worker, free, local)

**Short answer:** fixed by the S8 spell state that is already in run.py (take 1.5 s / join 3 s). No rule change.
Our two clips now give 216-288 sequences per team per 90 min (target 150-250; was ~1,100 per match).
On pro data with a ball dot that hops between players like ours, the same setting brings the count from 2.8x the
truth to 0.74x and the share of our sequences that are real from 22% to 53%.

## What was done
- The nightly Metrica check (tools/metricalab.py `ours()`) still counted sequences and balls lost on the RAW state,
  while the app counts them on the spell state since S8. It now mirrors run.py (`spell_take=0` = raw).
  Scorecard row has a note: expect ~7 pts fewer sequences found (47 -> 40%), not a regression.
- New noise in the Metrica check, `flicker`: the ball dot hops to the nearest opponent for 0.3-0.6 s
  (raw state agrees with the clean one on 78% of held frames, 17 team switches per minute). `flicker_heavy`
  (0.3-1.0 s, 48% agree, 28 per minute) brackets our clips (35-41 raw switches per minute, 4-5 on the spell state).
- tools/s6lab.py: 2 games x 2 halves, 4 noises, 6 spell settings. tests/test_s6_metrica_spell.py.

## Honest limits
- The spell state fixes the COUNT, not which sequences: under heavy flicker only ~1 in 5 of ours lines up with a real
  one (start within 2 s). Better sequences need a calmer ball/carrier (S8 a, the feet retrain running on Kaggle).
- On clean pro data (no flicker) the spell state costs 10 pts of sequences found (59 -> 49%): short real takeovers
  (< 1.5 s) are absorbed. Accepted, because our footage always flickers.
- Balls lost react strongly to the setting (flicker: 252 raw -> 300 at 1.5/3, truth 490); not graded here.
- No youth answer key for sequences; the per-90 target is a sanity range.
