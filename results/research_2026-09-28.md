# Research: how others do it + open-source we can reuse (28 Sep 2026)

## A. Commercial players (V = verified from source, I = inferred)
- Veo, patent US 11,310,418 (https://patents.justia.com/patent/11310418): per-pixel ball probability map; players detected and Kalman-tracked; player posture predicts where the ball is relative to the player; maps combined across players; particle filter (>=100 particles, Newtonian motion) picks the ball. V
- Veo blog (https://www.veo.com/en-us/article/the-road-to-simplicity-building-veo-go): "anything white would be recognized as a ball"; coloured pitches confused models. V
- Veo analytics: events are AI-detected and user-editable; sides, periods and lineup set by the user (https://support.veo.com/hc/en-us/articles/11873714080657). Shirt-number OCR 0-99 (https://support.veo.com/hc/en-us/articles/27698176364817). Only the follow-cam can be downloaded (https://support.veo.com/hc/en-us/articles/4451733961361). V
- Spiideo: AutoData from installed calibrated cameras; co-runs the SoccerNet SynLoc task (https://github.com/Spiideo/sskit). V/I
- Pixellot: DL separates players from referees/coaches/fans; manual field boundary at install (https://www.pixellot.tv/blog/behind-the-scenes-of-automated-production-how-does-it-work/). V (marketing)
- Trace: identity = jersey number + kit colour with the roster entered before the game (https://support.traceup.com/content/processing-faq-playerfocus). V
- Hudl Assist: human analysts tag football (https://www.hudl.com/products/assist/faq). V
- SkillCorner: detection, homography, tracking, recognition without pre-match training; off-screen players extrapolated, is_detected flag (https://github.com/SkillCorner/opendata). V
- Track160: teams and referees by jersey colour (https://www.track160.com/post/optical-tracking-the-holy-grail-of-tracking-data-in-football). V
- SoccerNet GSR 2024 winner (https://arxiv.org/html/2504.06357v1): referee = smallest of 3 embedding clusters of centre-pitch people; team side by mean-x vote; tracking in pitch coordinates. V

## C. Recommendations per problem
1. Black shirts labelled referee: treat detector output as "person"; roles per tracklet from per-match clusters (smallest = referee) + track vote. Longer term: fine-tune RF-DETR (Apache) on SoccerTrack v2 + our Veo frames (person, ball), which also removes the AGPL risk.
2. Dark players dropped: gta-link tracklet merging (MIT) + ByteTrack low-score/buffer tuning; detector fine-tune on night/dark-kit frames; track in pitch metres.
3. Referee kit per match: per-match clustering (done offline 28 Sep, ipanema/kits.py); optional one-click kit confirmation like Trace/Veo.
4. Ball: WASB heatmaps + player-position prior + particle/Kalman filter (Veo patent); fine-tune WASB on SoccerTrack v2 ball labels.
5. Events: possession state machine (FIFA rules), output SPADL via socceraction/kloppy, scored on SoccerTrack v2 / DFL labels; PathCRF as a ball-free cross-check.
6. New grounds: TVCalib (MIT) on our own line segmentation; NBJW/PnLCalib only as a research benchmark.

Not found: Spiideo/Pixellot/SkillCorner patent texts; any soccer detector without the black-referee bias; GTATrack licence; Kaggle DFL terms; StatsBomb/PFF commercial terms.
