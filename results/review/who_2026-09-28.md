Current model is right in 9 of 24 clear moments. What goes wrong (current model):
- 'dead ball' while the ball is in play: 5 moments (0, 12, 25, 31, 35) -> the dead-ball rule trusts off-pitch projections of
  wrong or airborne ball picks.
- a player clearly on the ball but no carrier found: 4, 7, 8, 29 (team still right by luck in 3) and 9, 18, 21 (dark on the ball -> 'loose').
- #2: carrier = white, model says dark (the smoothing overrides the carrier).
- ball pick itself wrong: on a player's head (#17), a spare ball by the post (#19), a player's body (#33).
Small key (24 moments): +-1 moment = 4 points. Next: grow it the same way (more strips, other grounds), then fix dead-ball and carrier linking against it.
