## What this says about "fix ball"
The remaining app misses (results/qa/ballmiss2: AIK 9/39, SFK-BP 5/34) are the finder scoring a shoe, a player or a
second ball above the match ball. More pictures (H1b) or re-weighting its own mistakes (HN1) did not change that.
Picker settings are exhausted (one-at-a-time sweeps, velocity model, slow off-pitch rule: no gain over AIK 30 / SFK 29 / B4 284).
Next ideas, not started: a second ball key on another ground so the 34+39 moments stop deciding everything (B6), and
labelling the look-alikes (shoes, second balls) in the miss pictures as explicit "not a ball" training examples.
