## What was used
- Ball positions: only the check pictures Daniel answered "yes" on Vasalund, Solheim, Spånga, Djursholm (65 balls;
  spare balls and "no" never used; Reymersholm and Solberga never used). Tests: tests/test_k1crops.py.
- The full label sets (thousands per match, labels/<match>_trainset_clicks.json) are only on the Modal volume, not in
  the repo or R2, so they could not be used without Modal.
- WASB soccer weights downloaded fine from Google Drive on Kaggle. But the pretrained WASB has no guess on the ball at
  47 of the 65 checked balls (the Modal runs used a WASB fine-tuned on SFK-BP, which is only on Modal), so following
  the ball through WASB gave only 160 balls (results/kaggle/ballcrops_k1/).
- K1b followed each checked ball +-12 frames by picture matching (start moved onto the ball, stop when it stops looking
  like a ball): 473 balls + 2,250 not-ball WASB guesses (results/free/k1b/, picture sheets there; ~5% of the ball
  pictures are wrong by eye, mostly on Vasalund).
- Grading as before: SFK-BP exam 81 frames + 34 clip moments, never trained on (tools/scorerlab.py, TAG=k1v1 / k1b).

## Why it probably fails
Other grounds look different (yellow balls on Spånga, night light), and most new ball pictures are not WASB guesses
while all not-ball pictures are, so the scorer can learn "how the crop was made" instead of "is it a ball".
A fair test needs the full checked label sets (on Modal) or the fine-tuned WASB on Kaggle.
