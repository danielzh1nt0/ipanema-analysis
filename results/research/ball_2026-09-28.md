# Ball tracking: deep research (28 Sep 2026) — summary; full sourced report in the session log
Key finding: 8 of our 13 misses have NO guess on the ball (at feet / crowd / far) -> the picker alone cannot fix them.
Ranked plan:
1. Possession state in the picker: at every frame add "ball at player P's feet" as a candidate (4-state model: in play / with a player / after possession / out; arXiv 2506.07981, 66% within 1 m on CPU). Free, CPU, test on the 34 moments. Check the Veo patent claims (posture-based ball inference) before shipping.
2. Dump raw finder responses below the cut-off at the 8 misses; top-K candidates, 2x2 tiling + flip TTA for far balls. Free on CPU.
3. Replace the YOLOv8 ball finder (AGPL-3.0) with RF-DETR (Apache-2.0) trained on a ball class. <$10 GPU.
4. Far more labels (target 5-10k frames; WASB soccer used ~12k): active learning (frames where the finders disagree), self-training on confident stretches, SoccerTrack v2 ball positions projected to pixels with the repo's fisheye script (ground balls only). Tens of $ GPU.
5. Temporal occlusion model (TOTNet / TrackNetV3 InpaintNet, MIT) after 4.
Evaluation: grow 34 moments to 300+, always report finder recall and pick accuracy separately, tag each miss by type, run as a nightly regression test.
