Pass Tapper

Four 2-minute clips (pick one above the video). Play a clip and tap the team's button at the moment a player kicks or heads the ball to a teammate, whether it arrives or not (free kicks and goal kicks count too). Don't tap shots, clearances, throw-ins or dribbling touches. Keys: S = SFK pass, O = opponent pass, Space = play/pause, Z = undo. Half speed makes it easier. Everything saves as you tap.
